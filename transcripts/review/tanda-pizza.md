### B) SYNTHESIS SCRIPT

You're looking at the digital front door for Tanda Pizza, an authentic Italian restaurant nestled among the rice paddies in Lovina, North Bali. If you've ever tried to manage a website in a place where reliable internet is often just a theory, you know the challenge: the site needs to work exactly the way the restaurant does, which means keeping things simple, fast, and remarkably robust.

The solution is a completely static, serverless website. There is no heavy CMS, no complex database, and no unnecessary bloat. It is designed to be lightweight so that it loads instantly even on the spotty mobile connections common in the area. Because the restaurant serves a diverse crowd of tourists, it offers full, runtime localization in five languages: English, Italian, Dutch, Indonesian, and Chinese.

The functionality goes well beyond a simple digital brochure. The site includes an interactive menu with a dedicated gluten-free toggle, making it easy for guests with dietary restrictions to find safe options instantly. It features a real-time status badge that tells you whether the restaurant is open, serving drinks, or closed, based on local time. Perhaps most importantly for visitors navigating rural Bali, it includes a digital driver card. With one tap, you can copy the restaurant’s address in Bahasa Indonesia or pull up a fullscreen view that you can show to a local driver, ensuring you actually make it to the restaurant without getting lost in the paddies.

Reservations are handled through direct WhatsApp integration, which is the primary way business is done in the region. Everything, from the live Instagram feed to the security headers configured for Cloudflare, is handled client-side. This project is a perfect example of matching the technical solution to the reality of the environment—no pretending to be a complex web application when a lean, fast, and hyper-practical tool serves the guests better.

If you want to see how this approach handles real-world constraints while delivering a polished experience, visit the live site at tandapizza.com.
