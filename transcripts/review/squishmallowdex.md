### B) SYNTHESIS SCRIPT

If you have a young collector at home, you know how quickly their passion for Squishmallows can grow. Tracking thousands of unique plush characters can be a challenge, so I built the Squishmallowdex. It is a dedicated, searchable collection tracker designed specifically for young collectors.

The goal was to create a tool that is as magical as the toys themselves, but also educational. It provides a clean, fast, and local database of over 3,000 creatures. Instead of relying on sites filled with ads or tracking, this project gives kids a private, offline-capable experience. They can browse the collection, filter by different criteria, favorite the ones they want, and mark the ones they already own.

The project is built to be a learning experience, too. It introduces the basics of data structure, showing how information is gathered and organized in a way that is easy to navigate. Once the initial setup is complete—using a simple, safe helper script—the collection works perfectly offline on a desktop, tablet, or mobile phone. There is no internet required to browse, no telemetry data being sent out, and no parent-hostile dark patterns to worry about.

Whether you are looking to help a child manage their collection or just want to see what a focused afternoon of development can produce, you can explore the project for yourself. Visit squishmallowdex.com to browse the live collection and learn more about how it works.
