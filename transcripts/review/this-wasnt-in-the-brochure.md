### B) SYNTHESIS SCRIPT

You packed for a picnic, but you ended up in the Drake Passage. You are the parent standing in the kitchen at 7:43 in the morning, muscles trembling after holding your child through a meltdown, thinking, nobody told me it would be like this. Or you are the co-parent sitting in the car after a school meeting, too exhausted to speak.

This Wasn't in the Brochure is a field guide for co-parenting children with ADHD, autism, PDA, and ODD. It is written not from the clinical sidelines, but from inside the storm. It bridges the gap between peer-reviewed neuroscience and the raw, sleep-deprived reality of raising a child whose brain doesn't follow the manual you were given.

The book uses a nautical metaphor because mainstream parenting advice is a map for calm water, and your family is sailing open ocean. You will learn why a meltdown is a biological power surge rather than a tantrum, why mornings tax the exact neural systems your child's brain under-produces, and why transitions between two homes can feel like switching operating systems.

There is also the double discovery. ADHD and autism are highly heritable. If your child is neurodivergent, the odds are high that you are too. The book treats this not as a complication, but as the foundation of effective co-parenting. When you understand your own brain, you stop fighting your nature and start scaffolding for it. You become a better navigator because you finally have your own map.

The book meets you where you are, with strategies for high-conflict or parallel parenting. Each chapter includes a field guide translating neuroscience into plain language, a parent toolkit with ten-minute exercises, and a survival card—a crisis protocol designed to be carried in your pocket for when your thinking brain goes offline.

By the end, you will have moved from being a bewildered tourist to a cartographer, capable of drawing your own maps and sailing with the wind. The book is available in four regionally localized editions, adapted to the specific educational and legal frameworks of the US, Australia, Britain, and New Zealand. You are not a failed neurotypical parent. You are an exceptional navigator in extraordinary conditions.

You are not alone. Learn more at thiswasntinthebrochure.wtf.
