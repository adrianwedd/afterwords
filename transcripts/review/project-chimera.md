### B) SYNTHESIS SCRIPT

You're looking at the reality of the post-model era. The frontier of artificial intelligence isn't just about the latest, most powerful model anymore. In fact, if you're still betting everything on a single proprietary model, you're looking at a structural liability. We’ve entered a cycle where frontier performance is just a temporary state, and the models themselves are rapidly becoming commodities.

If you want to know where the actual value is shifting, stop looking at the model labs and start following the acquisitions. Companies like Salesforce, Cisco, and Databricks aren't paying billions for models. They're investing in the messy, unglamorous plumbing—the data integration and governance that makes AI usable in the real world. Large enterprises are currently sitting on over a hundred siloed, disconnected software applications. You can have the best AI in the world, but if it can't navigate that fragmented architecture, it’s effectively useless.

This brings us to the model tax. Training these massive systems is expensive, and as they commoditize, the margins get thinner. Strategic advantage isn't found in simply throwing more compute at the problem. It's found in solving the last mile, which isn't a data pipe—it's a trust bridge. Legal, compliance, and security teams aren't going to let autonomous agents touch sensitive financial or customer data unless they have ironclad, auditable, and transparent oversight.

The shift is moving toward autonomous agents—systems that can perceive, reason, and execute complex, multi-step tasks. Academic and industry research is converging on this, focusing on agents for real-world business processes. This is creating a new category of software: Agentic Resource Planning. We're moving away from AI as a specialized tool for data scientists and into a future where it's a universal utility that needs to be tracked, governed, and managed.

The plumbing is the bottleneck, and whoever solves the governance problem wins the last mile. If you're interested in the future of enterprise AI, check out the full post on my site at adrianwedd.com/notebook/project-chimera.
