### B) SYNTHESIS SCRIPT

Most personal websites feel like museums. Everything is curated, chronological, and polished to perfection, while the real, messy work is locked away behind the scenes. This site is different. Think of it as a workshop. It is the place where I build things, think out loud, and occasionally break things in public.

I have been making software for a long time, and I have learned that my best work happens when I am not chasing a market, but chasing a genuine question. Whether I am building tools for audio archaeology, exploring AI safety, or putting together a tracker for my kids' toy collections, the process is always the same. I start with a question, I build until that question is answered or it evolves into something else, and then I write about what I found.

This site is my open log of that process. It is about technical craft, persistent curiosity, and designing for the inevitable failure modes that come with trying something new.

If you are just arriving, I recommend starting with one of my recent deep dives into AI model benchmarks, the architectural arguments for AI safety, or my thoughts on the vocabulary we use to describe machine cognition. You can find all of that, along with the ongoing experiment logs, right here.

The door is open and there is plenty more to come. You can find all the latest updates and projects at adrianwedd.com.
