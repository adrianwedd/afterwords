# Janet Fielding reference attribution

`voices/tegan-jovanka-ref.wav` is an excerpt adapted from **Janet Fielding oral history**, recorded September 2016, by **Spencer Howson**, collection 30716, John Oxley Library, State Library of Queensland.

Source: [State Library recording](https://hdl.handle.net/10462/mp3/1025).

The primary catalogue and digital viewer label the recording **[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)**. That licence is retained for this reference audio; the repository's software licence does not replace it.

Changes: continuous decoded PCM16 excerpt from 54.4 to 67.2 seconds, frames 2,611,200 through 3,225,600 (exclusive), at 48 kHz stereo. No filtering, normalization, separation, padding or concatenation. The accompanying transcript describes the actual retained speech, including its hesitations and repetition.

The publisher's catalogue states 1 hour 41 minutes 47 seconds; its delivered audio resource and the complete retrieved MP3 decode to approximately 1 hour 34 minutes 3 seconds. Both source records and the actual download hash are preserved in the evidence directory. This excerpt refers to that retrieved resource.
